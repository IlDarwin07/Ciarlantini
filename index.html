Woodquay · Galway

An espresso and a cannolo, the Italian way

Ciarlantini is a small Italian bar on Daly's Place. Proper coffee at the counter, a glass case of cannoli, cornetti and tiramisù, and the red front you can't miss on the way into town.

Al banco

Coffee

In Italy, the bar is where the day starts: a quick espresso standing at the counter, or a cappuccino and a cornetto if there's time. Here it's the same, just in Woodquay.

  • Espressoshort and strong
  • Macchiatoespresso, a spot of foam
  • Cappuccinothe morning classic
  • Americanolong and smooth
  • Lattehot or iced, oat milk too
  • Mochawith chocolate

To drink at the counter, at a table, or to take away.

La pasticceria

Italian pastries

The glass case is the reason most people come back. Crisp cannoli, tiramisù, cornetti and cakes, the kind you'd find in any pasticceria back home.

And the rest of the case

Cornetti in the morning, little pastries for the afternoon, and plenty to take away in a box. What's on the trays changes, so come and have a look.

Il bar

A little corner of Italy in Woodquay

Every Italian neighbourhood has one: the bar downstairs, where they know how you take your coffee and the cornetti are still warm. Ciarlantini is that bar for Woodquay. Stop for a minute at the counter, or stay a while with something sweet from the case.

  • 7:30Open early every day, for a coffee before work.
  • StayStools at the window, a few tables, and seats outside when the weather allows.
  • To goCoffee in a cup and pastries in a box, for the office or for dessert at home.

Look for the red building on Daly's Place, a couple of minutes' walk from Eyre Square.
